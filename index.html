DIGITAL LOGIC

How the Alarm Logic Works

The alarm activates for high temperature OR when the door is open AND the prolonged-open condition is active.

A = T OR (D AND L)
D — Door Open
L — Door Open Too Long
T — High Temperature
→
AND Gate
D AND L
OR Gate
T OR (D AND L)
→
ALARM
OUTPUT: 0

D = Door Open · T = High Temperature · L = Door Open Too Long · A = Alarm
